# Decision-Relevant Stage-0 Warrant Gate v1

## 1. Preserved diagnostic baseline

The accepted diagnostic branch `codex/unknown-fee-research-diagnosis-e1-e2-v1` was pushed to `origin` at `a520d09528c1b54ef74c39ed1fc9e5a0696afe0d`, and the remote SHA matched the local checkpoint before this milestone began.

## 2. Implemented warrant behavior

Stage 0 now asks whether a perfect external answer could change a merchant-relevant conclusion or permission. It no longer treats unresolved identity, beneficiary, retention, recurrence, component allocation, commercial reasonableness, or contract compliance as automatic research warrants. Every material fee receives a durable record of established evidence, unresolved fields, field-level decision relevance, merchant-document dependency, proposed research fields, the possible decision change, the final warrant decision, and explicit reasons.

## 3. Evidence reconciliation

The gate reconciles canonical statement structure and arithmetic, the Internal Analyst Finding, Open-World D1-D4, governed pricing/per-item/network knowledge, cardinality, and existing competing interpretations. A stronger supported upstream fact can satisfy a downstream determinant. A supported conflict is not silently resolved: it is stopped from external research and marked for governed domain adjudication.

## 4. Decision-relevance rule

Research is warranted only when a material unresolved non-contract field could change the current verification-only action, price-authority or spread conclusion, assessed-population conclusion, or another claim for which exact identity is explicitly necessary. A usable D1/D4 merchant conclusion stops research when remaining unknowns add no decision value. Merchant-specific compliance remains contract-dependent and is never assigned to public web research.

## 10. Cross-layer corrections and preserved conflicts

- Batch Settlement now inherits canonical complete per-item operands into Open-World D2 without claiming the printed count equals a separate settlement-batch table.
- AMEX NQUAL now inherits canonical rate-times-volume operands into Open-World D2 while bundled component allocation remains unresolved.
- Monthly Advantage now projects the printed rate-times-volume formula and proportional sensitivity. Canonical arithmetic remains unresolved because its normalized rate operand is still missing; no source fact was rewritten.
- Application Fee uses ordinary label structure plus Account Fees placement to support F7/current-period treatment and an N4 request without inferring recurrence or contractual authorization.
- Eighteen governed conflicts remain explicit and are routed to domain adjudication rather than external research or convenient winner selection. Sixteen were already signaled by competing-interpretation evidence; two additional rows expose a participant-role disagreement between governed layers.
