## 2. Exact behavior changed

- Statement-period reference resolution and current-reference maintenance are now separate outputs.
- Reference records carry an applicability start; future/current unresolved records cannot participate in older statement-period resolution.
- Prior dated values are projected as historical values, not as current values.
- Current-reference uncertainty is counted from the maintenance state, so the firewall does not erase it.
- The existing line-to-fee cardinality states are reused by the current-reference layer.
- Separately printed `AMEXCT### TOTAL` values are captured as reference-only evidence. They never enter the fee ledger or canonical totals.
- The Product authority and its fingerprint are attached to the new conflict rules and the revised Amex procedure.

## 3. Historical/current firewall

For every row, RateReveal now evaluates two claims:

1. the reference applicable to the statement's own period; and
2. the current maintenance reference as of the governed catalog date.

A current unresolved reference can remain visible in the second claim while the first claim stays closed or not applicable. In the 11-statement run, zero historical rows were reopened by current evidence. Ten current-uncertainty projections remain visible separately: nine Mastercard assessment rows and one Visa Base II Transmission row.

No historical-par claim is manufactured when period-matched evidence is absent. Processor/acquirer material remains scoped to the processor implementation and is not promoted to universal official-network par.
