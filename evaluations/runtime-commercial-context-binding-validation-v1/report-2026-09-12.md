- Fail-closed population correction: Explicit settled-transaction, authorization-attempt, approved-authorization, gateway-transaction, and settled-batch populations are compared as distinct identities and fail closed when incompatible.
- Normal report construction still uses fallback arbitration context; this validation does not attach new customer routing.
