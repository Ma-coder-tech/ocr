# Claim Verification and Evidence Calibration v1

Approved bounded offline evaluation. Preserve all existing implementation and dirty state. Use replay-admitted structural memberships and exactly replayed semantic records only. No new extractor, semantic grammar expansion, AI, authority, runtime integration, customer output, OCR, benchmark or multi-statement feature. Leave everything uncommitted.

Claims are explicit and narrow: a printed signed total matches its complete measure members; a claimed printed contribution union has a given value without duplicated source atoms; a printed contribution relation holds; two summaries identify the same/separate/overlapping economic activity; a fee basis phrase is directly supported; a fee calculation is sufficiently specified; a printed measure has the claimed role; or the whole financial population is complete.

Sufficiency is claim-specific and qualitative, not an invented confidence probability. Separate supported, contradicted, incomplete evidence, unresolved interpretation and invalid evidence/request. A contradiction requires valid admitted evidence and a decidable scoped assertion. Invalid/tampered evidence is not evidence against the financial claim. Incomplete observed runs never become complete populations through sum agreement. Different summaries, repeated values and equal totals do not prove economic identity. Combined amounts never silently split. Native export and same-statement control evidence are not independent financial corroboration.

Use exact signed integer monetary minor units or exact integer counts for additive checks; no epsilon, implicit absolute-value repair or rounding tolerance. Keep evidence members, source/control references, operands, signed residual, obligations, limits and allowed language in each result. Non-additive quantities remain unresolved. A printed fee basis observation is different from proof that a charge was correctly priced. Unsupported basis phrases remain visible and unavailable; units, sign and rounding remain unproven even where the phrase is parsed. A conditional literal product is diagnostic only and cannot pass a pricing claim.
